# Honest Person Assessments (بررسی صادقانه شخصیت)

## Trigger
User asks to analyze, "see", "read", profile, or psychically assess a REAL named person (e.g. "شخصیت خانم X را بررسی کنید / از طریق جزوه دیدن بررسی کنید") without verifiable data about them.

## Core principle
Never fabricate a personality/identity report about a real person. A claimed "reading" of a real human with no evidence is harmful fiction (can damage real people and mislead) and violates the user's own source ethics (صدق). Do NOT refuse the whole request either — the user wants execution, not lectures.

## Three honest routes (offer, or pick per context)
1. **Data-driven**: ask for real input (interview answers, questionnaire scores, observable behavior) → then deliver full analysis from that data, structure matching the requested method.
2. **Labeled hypothetical**: execute the complete method on a clearly labeled fictional case (badge "نمونه آموزشی — فرضی" on title slide AND footer of every slide; mark in the md too).
3. **Protocol + template**: deliver a structured assessment protocol (steps mapped to the method) + an empty-but-framed deck with placeholders like "در انتظار داده واقعی" badges, so the human fills in findings later.

## When user insists on performing the method anyway ("انجام بده")
Execute the method's FULL structure (its steps, dimensions, archetypes) but ground every finding in whatever real data exists; where data is absent, use ruled-out hypothetical content that is labeled or use "checklist to complete" cells. Keep the honesty note short and respectful — the deliverable matters more than the disclaimer.

## User-style preferences (Mina Kavoosi / Persian Telegram)
- Deliverable first, minimal commentary: "انجام بده، نظر رده", "بدون صحبت اضافی".
- More is better once asked: "بهتر و جامعتر" → escalate depth/quality on rerun.
- Respond in Persian; label hypothetical content explicitly (فرضی).
- The user's framework (علم متاآنتومیک "دیدن") is stated as a brain-powered skill, not supernatural: don't argue ontology, just don't claim you performed a magical action you cannot verify; frame your part as "processing/structuring" of what they perceive.
- Separate the user's own possible "seeing" (their brain) from your processing/sandboxing role: "شما ببینید، من ساختاردهی و مستندسازی کنم".

## Pitfalls
- NEVER present invented findings about a real identifiable person as real; label demo content every slide.
- A disclaimer paragraph is fine but never longer than the deliverable; avoid lecturing.
- Persian filenames → null-byte errors in terminal; copy to ASCII-safe path first.
- Use "فرضی" spelling without ZWNJ (U+200C) in memory/tool strings when needed.
